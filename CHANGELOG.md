  - The core changes. The pointer names its item by uuid, and the armed trigger names its item too.   - The writer and changes on disk. A document is written within a second, and at most once a second, with E-3's version cadence; kept versions are never written over. Folder edits, moves and deletes go to disk at once. A folder watcher reloads what arrives, keeping the three-second window for moves and the conflicts folder.
  - The live pointer. The engine keeps copies of the live list and playlist. Spec 7 §4's place rules apply when the live cue, item, list or playlist is gone, and everything that reads the pointer reads that place. The countdown sends `next` or `disarm` at its time. The live reading drives `/o/live/*`, `/o/live/armed`, `/o/query` and the `live` event.
