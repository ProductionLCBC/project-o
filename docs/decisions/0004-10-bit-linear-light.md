# ADR 0004: 10-bit, premultiplied, linear-light compositing from the start

Date: 2026-10-25 · Status: accepted

**Context.** The outputs are 10-bit SDI, and the colour and alpha policy had to be fixed before the first shader. Gamma-space blending (what ProPresenter and most switchers effectively do) is cheap and slightly wrong: dissolves dip, soft edges look heavy. Linear light costs a conversion on the way in and out and gets those right, and 10-bit output means 16-bit float intermediates anyway. **Decision.** Channel and stack textures are RGBA16Float, premultiplied alpha, linear light; transitions, grades and compositing run in linear light with a per-stack gamma-blend switch for legacy matching; every source converts on the way in and every sink encodes on the way out (v210 limited range on DeckLink, 10-bit full range on displays); key and fill ship premultiplied fill plus straight key, shaped by default; everything is SDR Rec.709, HDR out of scope; 1080i is produced by weaving a progressive render inside the DeckLink sink. **Consequences.** A 4096×2160 pass moves twice the bytes of an 8-bit pipeline, which Apple Silicon's unified memory handles but which is a standing reason to keep the number of full-frame passes small; dissolves will look brighter and cleaner mid-transition than ProPresenter's.
